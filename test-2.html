Mock Test 2

Harder on the calculation. More multi-step numericals and more questions whose data has to be converted before it can be used.

The paper

  • 180 minutes, 75 questions, 300 marks.
  • Three subjects. Each has Section A — 20 multiple-choice questions with one correct option — and Section B — 5 numerical-value questions where you type a number.
  • +4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong one, 0 for one left blank. Section B carries the same penalty as Section A, and all five of its questions are compulsory — that is the pattern in force since the 2025 session.
  • You may move between questions freely and change any answer until you submit. Nothing is marked until then.

What this paper is built from

The chapter mix is measured, not guessed: it follows the share each chapter took of the 2025 and 2026 papers held in this collection, which is the syllabus as NTA has actually examined it since the 2024 reduction. Chapters NTA dropped — Communication Systems in Physics, Surface Chemistry and metallurgy in Chemistry, Mathematical Reasoning in Mathematics — are absent, because they are absent from the 2025 and 2026 papers.

Once you start

  • The clock starts when you press the button and does not stop. If you close the page the clock keeps running; coming back offers you the time that is left, or submits what you had.
  • When the time runs out the paper submits itself.
  • After submitting you get your score, a percentile estimate, and every question worked through — right or wrong.

About the percentile. It is read off the published marks-against-percentile tables for JEE Main 2025, interpolated between their rows, and shown as a band because the real curve moves by a few hundredths between shifts. It assumes a cohort the size of 2025's. It is an estimate from a mock, not a prediction: no mock is calibrated against a real cohort, and an easy or hard shift moves everyone together. Read the band, not the digits.
